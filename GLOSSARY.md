# Samā4

A news reader that picks the day's most consequential stories and explains unfamiliar
names and ideas inline.

## Language

**Story**:
One news report from one publisher, as Samā4 shows it: headline, publisher, feed text,
brief and highlighted terms. The full report lives on the publisher's site.
_Avoid_: Article (in user-facing language), post, item

**Feed text**:
The publisher's own description of a story, taken from its RSS feed and shown unaltered.
It is all the publisher's text Samā4 shows; it is not the full story.
_Avoid_: Story text, article body, excerpt, content, teaser

**Brief**:
Samā4's own AI-written summary of a story, made from the headline and feed text. Always
whole sentences; a story whose feed text is too short to summarise has no brief.
_Avoid_: Summary (in user-facing language), blurb
