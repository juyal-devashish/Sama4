A log of an outside review of the live site, a critique ranked by severity, a market scan, five next moves and 100 tasks for the coding agent. Assumed context: solo, pre-revenue, aiming for 1,000 daily readers.

The reviewer never saw a story. Their fetcher gets server HTML only. It does not run JavaScript, click, type or resize. Today, Search and Outlook came back as an empty shell, so no stories were read and no explanations were opened.

Everything below comes from seven pages of HTML plus market research. Reading experience, context quality, typography, dark theme, contrast, speed and mobile layout were not assessed.

Part 4

Market gaps

The core feature, tap a term to get context, already ships in funded products.

Particle

Tapping an entity opens a page with a definition, stories, related entities and topics. Paid tier $2.99/mo or $29.99/yr.

Readwise Reader

Select any word or phrase for a lookup panel without leaving the page. $9.99/mo on the annual plan.

Google

AI summaries rolling out in Discover; testing AI article overviews on participating publishers' Google News pages.

Ground News

A different job: comparing coverage by bias. Annual plans $9.99, $29.99 and $99.99.

Perplexity

Facing suits from the New York Times, Dow Jones and CNN.

Semafor and Axios

Publishers, not readers. Semafor expects a second profitable year with live events about half of revenue. Axios Local relies mainly on niche professional advertising.

SmartNews

Launched NewsArc as an antidote to outrage content.

Apple

Reports say the new Siri will give daily news summaries from Apple News content. Nothing firmer found.

Top 5 next moves

  1. Decide whether samā4 is a reader or an extension, and rewrite the pitch to match what exists.Every other message depends on this.
  2. Put sources and dates on every explanation and measure accuracy on 200 samples.Verifiability is the one thing competitors do badly and samā4 can do alone.
  3. Server-render Today and story pages.Without this nobody can find, share or preview the work.
  4. Demote Outlook and strip its numbers until it can be scored.It adds risk without serving the core promise.
  5. Recruit 20 real readers and count explanations opened per story and day-7 return.This answers whether the context feature is useful before building voice or graphs.
